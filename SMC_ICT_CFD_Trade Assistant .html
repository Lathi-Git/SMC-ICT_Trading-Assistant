System Rules & Ratios

The fixed logic behind every checklist item. Nothing here is discretionary…

Instruments & priority

  • DXY first: analysed for dollar bias.
  • 1st priority: XAUUSD, GBPUSD, EURUSD — analysed if news events support the currencies.
  • 2nd priority: USDCHF, USDCAD, USDJPY — only when news for those currencies is present that day, 9:00–17:00 SAST.
  • Indices: US30, US100, S&P500 — at high-volume equity session opens.
  • Crypto (weekend): BTCUSD — only traded if balance exceeds R100.

Risk & scaling

  • Base margin: 0.01 lots for every R50 of balance. Under R50 = 0.01; R50–R100 = 0.02; continues in R50 intervals.
  • Stop loss cap: max 5 pips on standard FX, 50 pips on BTCUSD, at the adjacent Fib level.
  • Take profit: min 15 pips (150 on BTCUSD), between the -61.8% and -200% expansion.
  • Breakeven: a break of 0.0% Fib moves SL to entry immediately.

15-minute HTF alignment

  • Mark 00:00 Monday external high/low. Read structure on the line chart.
  • Plot 15m PSY levels: 0.1000, 0.0750, 0.0500, 0.0250, 0.0000.
  • Trend is valid only when the 50 SMA passes through the price body leading to the BOS, not just a wick.
  • Align FVG/OB with the SMA passthrough and PSY levels.

1-minute LTF execution

  • Entry gaps run hourly 07:00–18:00 SAST. First 15 minutes: LTF confirmation.
  • Golden period: 15–30 minutes past the hour.
  • Entry at 61.8% Fib overlapping the volume profile POC, a valid POI and a 1m PSY level (0.0875, 0.0625, 0.0375, 0.0125).

EMA/SMA & 15m candle close confirmation

  • Price must be at an Entry Gap (07:00–18:00 SAST) and have interacted with a PSY level; an interaction with the 50 EMA is then expected.
  • 50 EMA = thick red, 50 SMA = thin red, 200 SMA = blue. Bearish stack: 200 SMA > 50 SMA > 50 EMA. Bullish stack: 50 EMA > 50 SMA > 200 SMA.
  • Read the candle that touches or breaks the 50 EMA: bullish or bearish, and its intent (momentum, structure bias, MA formation, confluences).
  • Confirm Entry Gap and Golden Zone (61.8% + POC + POI + SMA/EMA zone + PSY), then trade at the POI.
  • Always confirm the candle with the Candlestick Pattern Document, and validate the 15m candle close (15m chart, during the Entry Gap or Golden Zone) against it.

BTCUSD / crypto module

  • Weekends or high volatility only, and only when balance exceeds R100.
  • Max SL 50 pips at 78.6% Fib, min TP 150 pips between -61.8% and -200% Fib, breakeven on the 0.0% Fib break.
  • Check USDT.D (Tether Dominance) as inverse context and flag divergence.
